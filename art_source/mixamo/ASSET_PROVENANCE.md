# Mixamo Vampire candidate provenance

- Service: Adobe Mixamo
- Character: `Vampire A Lusth`
- Download date: 2026-08-12
- Download account: user-authorized Adobe ID session
- Character download: FBX Binary, T-pose, with skin
- Motion downloads: FBX Binary, without skin, 30 FPS, no keyframe reduction
- Motions: `Standing Idle`, `Running` (in place), `Stable Sword Outward Slash`, `Standing React Large From Front`, `Dying`
- Adobe reference: <https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html>

The character and motions may be embedded in a game under the Mixamo terms. Raw Mixamo files must not be redistributed as standalone assets. The source FBX files remain under `art_source/mixamo/` for this private project and are deliberately excluded from the production `dist` output.
