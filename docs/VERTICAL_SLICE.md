# Ebon Veil: The Hollow March

## Original creative direction

An isometric dark-fantasy action RPG about a fallen duskwarden crossing a rain-soaked, moonlit ruin where an ancient bell has begun turning grief into living monsters. The slice is one dense biome—the Hollow March—culminating in a confrontation with **The Bell-Saint, Ossivar**.

This game uses an original setting, silhouettes, names, abilities, encounter layouts, materials, interface, audio, and procedural visual assets. Commercial isometric action RPGs are quality references only; no proprietary characters, story, maps, UI, assets, or distinctive presentation are reproduced.

## Player promise

- Immediate WASD locomotion with mouse-directed attacks, responsive dodge, generous input buffering, readable hit confirmation, and short recovery windows.
- Three transformations that alter traversal, defense, rhythm, and ability kit:
  - **Dreadwolf** — fastest movement, chained lunges, close-range predation, low poise.
  - **Wraith** — gliding movement, phase evade, ranged soul bolts, fragile body.
  - **Stonewrought** — slow and unstoppable, damage reduction, shockwave slam, high poise.
- A handcrafted route with layered sightlines, optional combat pockets, environmental storytelling, and landmarks that keep the player oriented.
- A small but complementary enemy roster: melee pressure, ranged zoning, evasive flanking, and support/control.
- One elite arena that tests transformation switching before a three-phase boss tests mastery of movement, timing, positioning, and form identity.

## Version gates

Each version is a complete implement → launch → playtest → render review → supervisor critique → fix → regression test loop. A version is not complete until evidence and remaining defects are recorded in `ITERATION_LOG.md`.

1. **Version 1 — The body:** input, camera, player controller, combat primitives, transformations, initial arena, live HUD.
2. **Version 2 — The hunt:** dense biome art pass, complete enemy roster, elite encounter, lighting, weather, effects, animation, procedural audio.
3. **Version 3 — The saint:** full multi-phase boss, phase presentation, arena mutation, victory loop, encounter readability.
4. **Version 4 — The edge:** measured performance, browser playtest, responsiveness tuning, accessibility/readability, supervisor defect burn-down.
5. **Version 5 — The showcase:** final art/feel/audio polish, regression, representative screenshots, checkpoint handoff.

## Quality bar and non-goals

The slice optimizes depth over content volume. There is no base building, crafting, farming, production chain, survival meter, or inventory grid. Browser constraints mean the goal is a polished, coherent original indie-commercial vertical slice—not a dishonest claim of AAA content scale.
