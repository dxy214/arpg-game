# Version 6 visual baseline

Evidence board: `evidence/v6-baseline-reference-board.jpg`. This board places official V Rising Steam screenshots beside the exact Version 5 checkpoint build that Version 6 inherits.

## Baseline verdict

**REJECTED — approximately 2.0/5 against the fixed commercial reference.** Version 5's 2.7/5 checkpoint score included gameplay, UI, boss-state integrity, and tested LOW-tier performance. The stricter Version 6 visual-only comparison is lower because the side-by-side frame exposes large production-art gaps.

| Category | V6 inherited baseline | Reference-comparison finding |
|---|---:|---|
| Environment composition | 2.4/5 | The Hollow March has an authored route and palette, but the reference sustains distinct foreground, playable midground, canopy/landmark background, and navigable negative space. Current detail disappears into one dark plane. |
| Terrain/materials | 2.1/5 | Generated ground breakup helps, but stones, graves, trees, armor, and water lack the reference's texture frequency range, edge wear, normal response, contact shadow, and material separation. |
| Character/enemy art | 1.5/5 | Current actors visibly read as assembled primitives. The reference retains anatomy, costume layers, weapon identity, and readable pose at comparable screen scale. |
| Animation/pose | 1.8/5 | Silhouettes change, but locomotion and attacks remain rigid; anticipation, weight transfer, contact, recovery, and secondary cloth motion are limited. |
| Skill VFX | 2.4/5 | Effects are readable and phase-colored, but lack bright-core/soft-edge/trail/debris/ground-response layering and convincing contact. |
| Lighting/value hierarchy | 1.9/5 | Current wide shots crush route and prop information into near-black. Reference dark scenes preserve local values, warm/cool accents, bounce, and focal contrast. |
| Boss spectacle | 3.0/5 | The boss has an original authored silhouette, phases, and honest hazards, but geometry/materials remain faceted and the arena is flat. |
| Camera/readability | 3.1/5 | Isometric framing and UI coexistence are coherent; current combat scale is readable but actors lack the visual information required by the framing. |

## Version 6 required corrections

1. Lift environment midtones selectively while retaining deep blacks only in non-playable framing areas.
2. Replace colored-cone enemy read with articulated, role-specific anatomy, equipment, and pose language.
3. Add material families and value separation to the player at actual gameplay scale.
4. Give core attacks and form skills a bright core, colored body, shaped edge/trail, and contact response.
5. Capture the fresh build at a matched route/combat scale and compare it beside the same fixed references. A source-only improvement does not close the version.

The quality target remains every visual category ≥4.5/5. Version 6 is expected to improve the largest gaps, not to claim that the full target has already been reached.
